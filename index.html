GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently
asked.

Have a question that isn’t covered here? Send us a message and we’ll help.

How do I book a car? +

Submit the enquiry form or message the business on WhatsApp. Your booking is not confirmed until the business responds and confirms it.

What documents should I carry? +

A valid driving licence and ID proof may be required. Ask the business which documents and formats it accepts.

Is fuel included in the rental price? +

Fuel policy can vary. Confirm whether the car is provided with a specific fuel level and how it should be returned.

Are there kilometre limits? +

Daily kilometre limits and extra kilometre charges are not set in this demo. Confirm the policy before booking.

Can I cancel or travel outstation? +

Cancellation charges and outstation permissions depend on the provider. Please confirm both before making plans.
